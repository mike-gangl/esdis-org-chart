## Purpose
Owns the lifecycle management of ESDIS data — from mission engagement and publication through long-term archival — and sets data governance standards and processes used across the ASSETs and ESSRP spheres.

## Deliverables
- Data lifecycle management across Mission Engagement, Data Publication, and Long-Term Archival
- Data governance products approved by the Data Standards Council (metadata standards, interoperability guidance, stewardship processes)
- Stewardship process documentation: MLP, DLP, Data Accession Process, LoS Model
- Data producer support and guidance (with ESDIS Connect)

## Members / Roles
- Leadership — Stewardship Lead (co-lead), Data Governance Lead, Mission Lifecycle Coordinator, Data Producer Support Coordinator
- Data Lifecycle Management
  - Mission Engagement — Mission Stewardship Leads (MSLs, grouped by ASSET), SOAR (Suborbital Archive Readiness Team)
  - Data Publication — Metadata & Document Curators, Data Engineers
  - Long-Term Archival
- Data Governance — Metadata Stewardship Team, Data Interoperability Team, Stewardship Processes Team, Data Standards WGs / tiger teams
- Data Producer Support — Guidance & Enablement, Needs-Driven Support, Long-Term Data Stewardship

## Dependencies
- Data Standards Council (approves Data Governance products)
- ESDIS Connect (Data Producer Support works with it)
- ASSETs (Mission Stewardship Leads grouped by ASSET)
