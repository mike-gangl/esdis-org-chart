## Purpose
Leads the ESDIS Project within the GSFC Flight Projects Directorate and reports to the NASA HQ ESDS Program. Sets overall project direction, owns risk/architecture decisions (via the DPM-S), and administers project resources (via the DPM-R).

## Deliverables
- Project Management Plan (PMP)
- Risk and opportunity register (DPM-S)
- Project budget and administrative support (DPM-R, Finance team)
- Chartering and oversight of governance bodies (Data Standards Council, ASSET Management Council, Enterprise Service Council, Ad Hoc Working Groups, Enterprise Portfolio Board)

## Members / Roles
- Project Manager (GSFC)
- Enterprise Project Scientist
- Deputy PM for Systems (DPM-S) — risk, opportunities, architecture
- Deputy PM for Resources (DPM-R) — supervises Finance; project admin support
- Administration team
- Finance team

## Dependencies
- NASA HQ ESDS Program (reporting line)
- GSFC Flight Projects Directorate (organizational home)
- FDP (Foundational Data Product) — DPM-S also serves as FDP's PM
